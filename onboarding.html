Automated Playback Accelerator & Intelligent Question Solver

Installation How It Works

Multiple Assignment Attempts Required

Do not use this extension if your assignment only allows a single attempt. On the first run, the extension uses a guess & try approach that will get questions wrong while discovering correct answers. You need multiple attempts on the assignment to retake it and achieve a full score. (The bulb itself does not need unlimited attempts, but your Canvas assignment must allow multiple attempts).

1 How It Works: Guess & Try

The extension uses a two-step process to solve questions and complete assignments:

Attempt 1

Phase 1: Guess & Try Run

On the first attempt, the extension does not know the correct answers yet.

  • It guesses answers and checks feedback to determine which choices are correct.
  • Getting questions wrong on this attempt is expected.
  • When correct answers are revealed, the extension saves them to local memory.
  • It filters out known wrong combinations so it never repeats a mistake.
Attempt 2+

Phase 2: Full Score Run

Once Attempt 1 completes, the extension automatically starts a retake.

  • The extension recalls the saved correct answers from the first attempt.
  • It inputs and submits the correct answers immediately.
  • The assignment completes with a 100% score, halts playback, and plays a completion chime.

2 How to Install (Chrome Developer Mode)

Since this tool is loaded directly into Google Chrome as an unpacked extension:

1

Open Chrome Extensions

In Google Chrome, navigate to chrome://extensions in your address bar.

2

Enable Developer Mode

Turn ON the Developer mode toggle located in the upper-right corner of the page.

3

Load Unpacked Extension

Click the Load unpacked button in the top-left corner, and select the folder containing this extension (Playposit_yeeter_v3).

Important: You must keep this folder on your computer. Do not delete or move the folder after installing—Chrome runs developer extensions directly from their disk location. If the folder is deleted or moved, the extension will stop working. (A packaged .crx file will be provided later).
4

Pin to Toolbar

Click the puzzle piece icon on your Chrome toolbar and pin WeVideo (Playposit) ToolKit for 1-click access.

3 FastStep Modes & Network Buffering

Why doesn't the extension just jump straight to the end of the video?

Why Full Skipping Can Fail: PlayPosit streams videos across third-party CDNs (WeVideo, YouTube, Kaltura, Vimeo). Media streams require chunked video buffering. If an extension blindly jumps to the end, the player can freeze, trigger network timeout errors, or crash the PlayPosit heartbeat. FastStep offers three calibrated pacing modes in the Settings panel:

Legacy

Most Stable

Standard 5-second skip intervals every 90ms. Provides maximum stability on slower internet connections or heavy video streams.

Balanced

Recommended

Accelerated 10-second skip intervals every 45ms with hardware-accelerated timeupdate sync. High speed while protecting buffer state.

Super

Experimental

Forces the video timeline toward the end. PlayPosit's internal listener catches the jump and stops at the upcoming question point.

4 Auto-Initialize & Canvas URL Safety

Auto-Initialize is OFF by Default: To protect non-assignment pages and other video players, auto-initialization is disabled by default.

Toolbar Activation: When Auto-initialize is OFF, simply click the WeVideo (Playposit) ToolKit icon in your Chrome toolbar on any assignment tab. The badge will illuminate to ON and immediately inject the control panel into the page and its embedded video player frames.

Do Not Whitelist Your Generic Canvas Domain

Canvas uses HTML5 video players across many pages: instructor lectures, Panopto/Studio recordings, Zoom archive embeds, and announcements.

If you configure a generic domain like canvas.example.edu as an activation URL, the ToolKit will activate on non-PlayPosit video players across Canvas tabs.

Recommended URL Format: Include Specific Course Numbers

Specify the exact Canvas course number where your PlayPosit bulbs are assigned:

canvas.example.edu/676767

You can add multiple courses by separating them with commas in the Settings panel.

Why Not Restrict URLs in the Chrome Manifest? Chrome extension manifests declare host permissions statically at install time. Extensions cannot rewrite their own manifest.json dynamically based on custom user inputs without prompting a full extension reload and permission reinstall. Instead, the ToolKit utilizes Chrome's native activeTab API: with Auto-Initialize OFF, the extension runs only when you explicitly click its toolbar icon, giving you full control and avoiding unwanted page interference.

5 UI Controls, Status History & Chimes

Audio Chimes

  • 100% Victory Chime: Plays when the assignment reaches 100% completion and halts playback.
  • Retake Chime: Plays when an attempt is reset for another run.
  • Interaction Chime: Alerts when playback pauses for a question, with rate-limiting to prevent repeated ringing during question series.

Status History

The status bar displays a scrolling history of the last 4 actions with timestamps so you can see what the solver is doing:

[15:52:10] Trying Known Answer
[15:52:11] Submitting
